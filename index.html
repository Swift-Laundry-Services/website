Frequently asked questions

How do you care for your laundry?

Every order is sorted, stains are pre-treated, and your laundry is washed, dried and neatly folded before it comes back to you. If you have sensitive skin or prefer greener products, ask for our eco-friendly or hypoallergenic detergent options.

What are your turnaround times?

You can get your laundry back within as little as 24 hours. Let us know when you book if you need it back by a certain day.

Pricing & payment?

Wash & fold is priced by the bag: $41.99 for a half bag (1–10 lbs), $56.99 for one full bag (10–25 lbs) and $79.99 for two full bags (25–40 lbs). Any additional weight is $1.90/lb, and we weigh your order at pickup. See all prices on our Services page.

We accept cash, card, Apple Pay, Zelle and PayPal.

Pick-up / drop-off?

Pickup and delivery are handled directly by our own team — not third parties. We serve Garfield Heights and the Cleveland suburbs, with weekly, bi-weekly or one-time pickups. Request a pickup or call us on (714) 402-7530.

Do you serve my area?

We serve Garfield Heights and the Cleveland suburbs. Not sure if your street is covered? Give us a call and we’ll let you know.

Are there any limitations?

Some items need special handling — for example very large or heavy items, or laundry that is unsafe or unsanitary to process. Please call us on (714) 402-7530 before your pickup and we’ll let you know what we can take.
